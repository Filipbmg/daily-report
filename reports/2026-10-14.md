**Repeat buying and broader insider pattern:** Cohen has purchased **4,144,810 shares for approximately $97,192,416 since September 10**, weighted at **$23.4492**. The five CEO purchase dates are September 10 (1,000,000), September 21 (1,150,680), September 29 (450,000), October 2 (700,000) and October 13 (844,130). The first four are *prior context*; **only the October 13 filing is newly eligible today**. Directors Lawrence Cheng, James Grube, Alain Attal and Nat Turner also made separate purchases since September 8, totaling about **$1.99M**. This represents five distinct buyers across the broader period, **not** a coordinated same-day C-suite cluster. Small officer sales, including by principal financial officer Daniel Moore and general counsel Mark Robinson, are a counter-signal but much smaller in dollar terms.

**Vehicle and price:** Finviz at **08:45 ET** indicated **$27.38**, about **+0.59%** versus the October 13 close of **$27.22**, **+1.48% versus the fresh $26.980 entry** and **+16.76% versus Cohen's five-purchase $23.449 basis**. Market capitalization is roughly **$13.8B**, with **412M float**, **42.67M shares short / 10.36% short float**, short ratio **5.32**, beta **1.66**, 14-day ATR **$0.96 / ~3.53%**, and reported recent volatility of approximately **4–5%**. GME is larger than our ideal vehicle, but its options activity, retail reflexivity, short base and repeated 5% sessions make a 5–10% day plausible. The stock is up about **27.7% over a month** and RSI is around **75**, so it is also extended.

**Participation:** October 13 volume was approximately **12.806M** shares versus Finviz's **~8.03M** normal average, or **1.59x**. The last three completed sessions (Oct. 9, 12, 13) averaged approximately **12.738M**, also **1.59x**. This is supportive because the stock has been rising, but it is not an explosive 5x volume dislocation. Finviz showed about **175K premarket shares** at 08:45 ET; a reliable normal premarket baseline was unavailable, so no premarket ratio is asserted.

**Why Cohen may be buying:** The confirmed fact is repeated, increasingly expensive personal accumulation. A possible interpretation is conviction in GameStop's balance sheet and management's capital-allocation strategy, not a confirmed pending takeover. September's earnings showed improving profitability but continued declining sales. Speculation around a potential eBay deal or partnership is **not** a confirmed scheduled catalyst.

**Upcoming event and dilution:** GameStop's outstanding **$32 cash-exercise warrants expire October 30, 2026**. The company registered up to about **59.15M** shares issuable on exercise, potentially bringing up to **~$1.9B cash** if fully exercised. This is an important potential dilution and event-structure overhang, **not proof of present selling or an active common-stock offering**. At the current price the warrants are out of the money. Convertible notes due 2030/2032 are another capital-structure consideration. An August 2026 amendment settled approximately **$358.4M** of a note exchange in cash rather than stock, reducing one source of potential issuance. No newly announced common-stock financing tied to Cohen's purchase was identified.

**Why it could pop today:** A verified **$22.8M fresh CEO open-market buy** extending nearly **$100M** of personal purchases is appearing in a high-attention, optionable stock with above-normal rising-price participation and meaningful short interest. This is exactly the sort of filing that can be amplified by retail momentum. **Watch $27.00 (fresh insider reference), $27.22 (prior close), $27.53 (52-week high) and $28.00**. A break above $27.53 on sustained opening volume would be more persuasive than an isolated premarket uptick.

**Risks:** The stock already rallied substantially; $13.8B capitalization means the filing alone may not move it 10%; the premarket response was only about +0.6% at the observed time; warrant-related positioning may add volatility in both directions; operating sales remain pressured. **Do not assume that a large buy guarantees a squeeze.**
