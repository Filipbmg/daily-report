## Decision and limitations

**First watch: FUN.** The ~$14.9M H Partners accumulation is the most substantial clean newly reported buying in a stock with demonstrated 5–16% sessions, although Tuesday's high-volume decline and ~$4.66B net debt are material negatives. **Second: SLQT**, where the CEO/COO/president/director cluster is more independently informative but the penny-stock capital structure, weak participation and liquidity make execution considerably riskier. **Third: WW**, where a genuine CEO/director cluster and ~10% daily moves offer an alternative if volume persists. **ETRA** is the cleaner biotech specialist accumulation; **ATEC** and **DTI** are lower-confidence reversal plays.

**No trade is automatic.** Require price/volume confirmation, verify fresh premarket indications and spreads, and re-check the latest EDGAR filing/financing before entering. No definitive SEC-wide exhaustive check of every financing document or verified live premarket quote was available for every candidate, and some three-month volume denominators are unavailable. Those gaps are disclosed rather than filled with guesses.
