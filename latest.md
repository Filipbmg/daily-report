## #1 DKS — DICK'S Sporting Goods

This is not normally the sector I'd want at #1. Today, however, the actual tape and insider pattern justify the exception.

EVP and CFO **Navdeep Gupta bought 7,707 shares at a weighted $129.75 for $999,983**. Director **Larry Fitzgerald Jr. bought another 1,860 at $131.67 for $244,906**. The filings confirm transaction-code-P common-stock purchases, with no 10b5-1 election indicated.

Fresh-window totals:

- **9,567 shares**
- **$1.245M**
- **Weighted average = ~$130.13**

But the broader accumulation is considerably more interesting.

Following the huge August earnings collapse, directors Mark Barrenechea, William Colombo, Robert Eddy and Sandeep Mathrani collectively bought millions of dollars of stock around $128.70-$130.72. Adding Gupta and Fitzgerald brings the recent accumulation to roughly:

- **39,130 shares**
- **$5.09M**
- **Weighted average ≈ $130.05**

So the current $133-ish stock is still only around **2.5% above the cost basis of an unusually broad insider cluster**.

### Why DKS passes the volatility gate

Published beta is only about 1.14, but that understates the current realized behavior for this strategy.

Recent tape includes:

- **Sep. 15: -5.39%**
- **Sep. 22: +8.34%**

The stock also collapsed roughly 30% following the August earnings/guidance reset and remained down roughly 44% over three months at report time.

The float is around 63M shares with roughly **13% short interest**, which adds some reflexivity if the recovery gains traction.

Thursday volume was about **2.46M** versus a roughly **2.96M 60-day average**, approximately **0.82x**. The latest three sessions average roughly 3.08M, approximately **1.03x normal**.

So volume is **not yet confirming a breakout**. That's the main weakness.

### Why they may be buying

This looks much more like **senior insiders disagreeing with the magnitude of the post-Foot-Locker repricing** than knowledge of a specific imminent event.

The August earnings shock came largely from Foot Locker integration economics and revised guidance. Four directors initially bought approximately $3.7M immediately after that collapse. Now the CFO himself is committing another **$1M**.

That progression matters:

**Directors buy the crash → stock stabilizes → CFO commits $1M → another director adds.**

The CFO is particularly relevant because the market's concern is fundamentally about **earnings, margins and integration economics**.

### Why this could pop today

The insider signal is much stronger than a routine director buy, the stock has demonstrated an 8% upside session literally this week, short interest isn't trivial, and current price remains close to the ~$130 cluster basis.

**The $129.75-$131.67 area is the important insider zone.**

If DKS attracts increased participation while remaining above that area, the setup improves materially.
